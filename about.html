About

B2B marketing for purpose-driven teams

Hey, I’m Roy.

I help B2B SaaS companies turn early traction into scalable, repeatable growth through sharp positioning, full-funnel content, and demand gen that actually converts. I’ve built and led content and demand-gen engines across SEO, SEM, email, social, and partnerships — and aligned marketing with sales and product-led growth so the metrics move together.

That training taught me to think clearly, argue persuasively, and focus on what matters — and it still shapes how I approach everything from product positioning to campaign design. I thrive where the playbook isn’t written and results matter more than theory.
